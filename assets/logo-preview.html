🎨 Philosophy of the Design (Why it doesn't look AI-generated):

  • No Cheap AI Cliches: No 3D metallic chrome reflections, no generic cyborg robot dogs, and no clip-art cartoon cats with headphones.
  • Pure Geometric Semantics: The 4 toe pads are designed as harmonic audio frequency capsules (tuned to the 250Hz, 1kHz, 2kHz, and 4kHz domestic vocalization bands).
  • The Negative-Space Hertz Wave: Cutting across the central metacarpal pad is a clean, mathematical Fundamental Frequency (F0) sine wave.
  • Color Palette: Electric Cyan (#22D3EE) transitioning into Deep Indigo/Violet (#6366F1) — evoking signal precision, digital audio workstations (DAW), and modern developer tooling.
